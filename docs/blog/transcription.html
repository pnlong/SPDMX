Experiment · Transcription

Multi-instrument transcription

Question

Because SPDMX pairs mixtures with MIDI, can it support multi-instrument automatic music transcription under a fixed budget, and how does training on SPDMX compare to Slakh2100-redux in-domain and across domains?

Setup

  • Model: YourMT3-style encoder–decoder (T5-small), trained from scratch.
  • Input: mixture audio with aligned MIDI.
  • Arms: Slakh2100-redux vs SPDMX songs with ≥2 stems.
  • Budget: 100,000 matched steps.
  • Eval: official Slakh2100 test split and a held-out SPDMX split.

Metrics

  • Onset F1 (↑): instrument-agnostic; non-drum notes; pitch and onset within ±50 ms; offsets ignored.
  • Multi F1 (↑): includes drums; requires pitch, onset, offset, and instrument matches (drum offsets ignored).

Absolute scores remain low by design: small training budget and no pretrained MT3-style recipes. This is a fixed-budget alignment and domain-gap check, not a comparison to published systems.

Results

Fixed-budget multi-instrument transcription results. Track mean ± 95% CI.

On the Slakh2100 test split, Slakh- and SPDMX-trained models reach onset F1 near 0.07 under equal training budgets. On the SPDMX test set, Slakh-trained models are much worse (0.041 onset / 0.010 multi) than the in-domain model (0.265 onset / 0.191 multi), indicating that Slakh training may show lower generalization than training with SPDMX under this setup.

Caveat: cross-domain transfer remains limited under this budget; scores are not comparable to published MT3 systems.
