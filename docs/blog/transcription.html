Multi-instrument transcription

Question

Holding a small YourMT3-style model fixed, does SPDMX multitrack support in-domain automatic music transcription, and how does it compare to Slakh2100-redux under matched steps?

Setup

  • Model: YourMT3-style encoder–decoder (T5-small), trained from scratch.
  • Input: mixture audio with aligned MIDI (MT3 / YourMT3 mixture→multi-instrument setup).
  • Arms: Slakh2100-redux | SPDMX songs with ≥2 stems | optional union / hour-matched SPDMX subsample.
  • Budget: 100k matched steps.
  • Eval: official Slakh2100 test split and a held-out SPDMX split.

Metrics

  • Onset F1 (↑): instrument-agnostic; non-drum notes; pitch and onset within ±50 ms; offsets ignored.
  • Multi F1 (↑): includes drums; requires pitch, onset, offset, and instrument matches (drum offsets ignored).

Absolute scores are intentionally low: small budget and no pretrained MT3 recipes. This is an alignment and domain-gap check, not a comparison to published MT3-style systems.

Results

Matched-step multi-instrument transcription (T5-small from scratch, 100k steps). Track mean ± percentile-bootstrap 95% CI half-width.
Train Test Onset F1 ↑ Multi F1 ↑
Slakh2100Slakh21000.072±0.0050.083±0.006
Slakh2100SPDMX0.041±0.0010.010±0.001
SPDMXSlakh21000.075±0.0060.066±0.006
SPDMXSPDMX0.265±0.0060.191±0.006

On Slakh test, Slakh- and SPDMX-trained models reach onset F1 near 0.07 under equal update budgets (this does not isolate training-hour effects). Cross-domain Slakh→SPDMX is weaker (0.041 onset / 0.010 multi). On held-out SPDMX, the in-domain model reaches 0.265 onset F1 — usable in-domain audio–MIDI supervision within the rendered domain.

Caveat: cross-domain transfer remains limited under this budget; scores are not comparable to published MT3 systems.
