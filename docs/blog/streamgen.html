StreamGen accompaniment

Question

Does training stream-music-gen (Wu et al., 2025) on SPDMX multitrack beat the Slakh baseline under matched steps?

Setup

  • Config: dec_online, future_visibility = 0.
  • Arms: Slakh | SPDMX multitrack | optional union / hour-matched SPDMX.
  • Data prep: DAC → RMS → mixdown via prepare_streamgen_data.sh; SPDMX adapter under experiments/streamgen/adapters/.

Metrics

  • Primary: Slakh-test COCOLA, Beat F1, FAD.
  • Secondary: SPDMX held-out.

Domain gap is a known risk (as in the Demucs experiment): strong in-domain numbers need not imply Slakh-test or recorded transfer.
