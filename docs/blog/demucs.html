Matched-budget Hybrid Demucs

Question

Under a matched step budget, does SPDMX BDGP provide usable Hybrid Demucs supervision versus Slakh2100, and how does performance transfer across synthetic and recorded domains?

Setup

  • Model: four-source Hybrid Demucs, trained from random initialization.
  • Targets: bass, drums, guitar, piano (BDGP). SPDMX stems map from GM program ranges; Slakh from dataset metadata. Keep only songs that contain all four targets.
  • SPDMX pool: 632 BDGP-eligible songs → 600 train / 32 validation (song-level shuffle).
  • Arms: Slakh alone | SPDMX alone | Slakh ∪ SPDMX (union), sampled uniformly by song.
  • Budget: 32k updates, batch size 8, 10 s crops (mono duplicated to stereo). Equal updates control compute, not unique-song exposure.

Metrics

Primary: SI-SDR (dB, ↑), ignoring overall loudness. Report bass–drums (BD) and guitar–piano (GP) means separately. Eval on Slakh2100 and SPDMX held-out mixtures, plus recorded sets where targets map cleanly: MUSDB18 (BD only), MoisesDB, and MedleyDB (union of MedleyDB and MedleyDB 2.0).

In domain, BD is strong on the matching corpus (~10 dB), while GP remains several dB lower (guitar weakest). Across synthetic domains and on recorded tests, BD stays near zero or mildly positive (best MoisesDB union BD at +2.4 dB), but GP falls to roughly −4 to −12 dB. Union retains most in-domain BD on both synthetic sets and has the highest recorded BD point estimates on MoisesDB and MedleyDB.

Caveats: single matched-step run (no training-run uncertainty); not a scaling study on the full corpus; pitched-stem transfer to recordings remains weak. Treat this as an alignment / domain-gap check, not a separation SOTA claim.
