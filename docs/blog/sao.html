Stable Audio Open fine-tunes

Question

Do matched-step Stable Audio Open (SAO) fine-tunes on SPDMX mixtures improve over Slakh under FAD and CLAP, and does the full corpus beat the BDGP-matched subset?

Setup

  • Model: Stable Audio Open fine-tunes (pretrained checkpoint).
  • Arms:
    • slakh — Slakh train mixes
    • spdmx_matched — songs with subset:bdgp (same BDGP pool as separation)
    • spdmx_full — all SPDMX songs with a shipped mix
  • SPDMX mixes are mono; SAO expects stereo — stable-audio-tools duplicates L/R at load time (do not store stereo mixes in the release).
  • Index shipped mix.flac paths (chunked release or flat SPDMX_dev/mix/); prefer songs.csv with lengths and subset flags.

Metrics

  • VGGish / OpenL3 FAD on held-out generations (reference-sensitive: Slakh-like acoustics are favored when refs are Slakh mixes).
  • LAION-CLAP scores on held-out generations.

Results

Metrics and audio examples will land here when the pilot finishes (sao_metrics.csv).

Caveat: FAD depends strongly on the reference set; treat Slakh-referenced FAD as a domain-similarity signal, not a pure quality ranking for SPDMX-trained models.
