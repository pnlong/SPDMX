Soundfonts

SPDMX renders each stem with a locked per-category recipe: Basic, Varied, MIDI-DDSP with a Basic fallback, or MIDI-DDSP with a Varied fallback. Basic uses one FluidSynth bank (SGM-V2.01); Varied samples a listening-selected shortlist. MIDI-DDSP recipes try neural synthesis first and fall back to the listed banks when a stem is ineligible. Open the dropdowns for bank names.
